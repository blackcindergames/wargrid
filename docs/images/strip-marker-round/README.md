# Strip marker round pictures

Source for the War Room round `strip-marker` (P16, 2026-11-23). `marker.html` draws the top strip with the production strip's shipped upright chip (42 x 26, columns 2 | 9 count | 2 | 18 glyph | 4 | 4 x 20 bar | 3, as `game/scripts/match/production_strip.gd` lays it out) at 1280 x 720 over `../hud-v5/source/worlds/w-factory.png`, with the round's variants as hash or query parameters: `state` = `split` (4 Medium Tanks, then 3 Rotary Gun Tanks), `alt` (the two alternating, 6 in all) or `single` (7 Medium Tanks) for the selected Vehicle Factory; `marker` = `none`, `glyph`, `kinds`, `plus` or `pips` (options A to E); `card=1` puts the pointer on the factory chip with its hover card. The chip's number is the head kind's total in every option. The page needs the repository layout (`../hud-v5/source/glyphs3.js`, `game/fonts/`); War Room renders it live. The `.webp` files are 2x exports at 2560 x 1440 of the `split` queue, one per option (`marker-<letter>`, `marker-a-card` for A's hover card); `marker-<letter>-1x` are 1280 x 720 shots cropped to the step's crop (520 x 140 at 760, 0) and enlarged 200 % with nearest-neighbour (`magick -filter point -resize 200%`), so the owner judges 1x pixels. All were shot with headless Firefox from `python3 docs/images/hud-v5/source/server.py` and converted with `cwebp -lossless -z 9 -exact`. The answered production strip round keeps its own page in `../strip-round/`.
