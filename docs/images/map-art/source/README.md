# Map art: source

The page and scripts the pictures in `docs/images/map-art/` and the HUD v5 backdrops are made from. The page draws the HUD layer with the shared HUD file `../../hud-v5/source/hud.js` and `hud.css`. Every picture is a vector redraw of the game's exported shapes, so any of them can be re-made at 2560 x 1440 without upscaling.

Needs: Firefox at `/Applications/Firefox.app`, `python3`, `cwebp`, ImageMagick (`magick`, `compare`) for proofs, Godot (`$GODOT_BIN` or `godot`) for the shape export and the round trip. A missing tool may be installed with Homebrew or `uv` (Pillow only as `uv run --with pillow python3 ...`).

Run from the repository root: `python3 docs/images/map-art/source/server.py` serves the repository root on 127.0.0.1:8769. Open `http://127.0.0.1:8769/docs/images/map-art/source/round6.html` in Firefox for the round 6 pick page (from 1760 px of window width the text panel sits right of the stage, the buttons, note and navigation stay in view at 1920 x 1080; narrower windows stack); `#list` lists the pictures.

Hashes (the page never shows anything else on a stage):

- no hash: the pick page. One step at a time on the base scene (sand, zoom 0.5, lifted owner colours, HUD v5 layer off). Keys: `A`/`B` flip, `Enter` picks (a step with one drawing: `Enter` is "yes", the Change button sends `change` with the note), `H` HUD v5 layer, `G` sand or dark ground, `Z` zoom 0.5, 1, 2, `[` `]` heading, `N` `P` next and previous step. A pick posts `{key, pick, note}` to `/pick`; the page says `Recorded.` only on a 204, else `Not recorded`. Without `source/picks.json` every step shows its `rec` option. `#step=<n>` opens step n.
- `#step=<n>&show=A|B|0&ground=sand|dark&zoom=0.5|1|2&shot=1[&scale=2]`: that step's stage only (`0` is today); `#pic=step<n>-<A|B>[-dark]` is the same and shoots with `shoot.sh`, at the zoom of the step's `pics.zoom` (else 0.5; `EXTRA='&zoom=1'` overrides). These names are not in `#report=pics`.
- `#pic=<name>&as=shot|final&scale=1|2`: the stage only, 1280 x 720 times `scale` pixels. `shot` is the selection the committed picture was shot with, `final` the selection for the re-shoot. A `PICS` entry with `hud: '<state>'` draws the HUD v5 layer of that state over its stage, as `&hud=<state>` does for any picture.
- `#world=<state>&scale=2`: a HUD v5 world backdrop, 1280 x 800 times `scale` pixels (states `none one many factory plant builder build place blocked enemy pile site`), in the round 6 look with its marks; shot with plain headless `--screenshot` at 2560 x 1600.
- `#export`: posts the picked shapes to `../shapes-picked.json` (`sh export.sh`). `#roundtrip[&set=today]`: compares `build/map-art/roundtrip-godot.json` with the page's own drawing (`sh roundtrip.sh`). `#audit`: paint order, footprint, owner share, contrast and colour-vision checks over every picked shape (`sh report.sh audit audit`).
- `#report=fonts`, `#report=pics`: post a text report to `build/map-art/<name>.txt` (`&name=<[a-z0-9-]+>` sets the file name). The last line starts with `PASS` or `FAIL`.
- `&font=inter` or `&font=arial` (proofs only): draw text in Inter from `build/map-art/proof-fonts/` or in Arial.

| `marks.js` | `MARKS`: the white map marks, the twelve `#world` backdrop scenes and the `marks-z1` and `gate-reference-z1` stages, drawn with the page's functions in the baked round 6 look |
| `shoot_worlds.sh` | `sh shoot_worlds.sh [state ...]`: the HUD v5 backdrops from `#world`, 2560 x 1600 PNG into `docs/images/hud-v5/source/worlds/` (needs the server) |
| `page.css` | Page styles and the four Public Sans faces from `game/fonts/` |
| `server.py` | Static server; `POST /pick` writes `picks.json` (ignored by Git), `POST /export` writes `../shapes-picked.json`, `POST /report?name=` writes `build/map-art/<name>.txt` |

A selection is `{steps, fix4b, fix6}`: one option per step of rounds 1 to 6 (`'0'` is today, a step left out does not apply). `ALL_PICKED` and `ALL_TODAY` are the two extremes. The rounds 1 to 4 pictures keep their own ground and shadow drawing (`legacy` in the selection), so they still match the pictures as committed.

## Round 6 step files (the contract for the drawing agents)

One file per step, `cand6-<key>.js`, key in `surfaces`, `edge`, `power`, `owners`, `cargo`, `paused`, `wrecks`. It sets `CAND6.<key> = {q, why, look, rec, recWhy, numbers?, view?, pics?, A: {name, data}, B?: {name, data}}`. Step files load before the page's functions: compute nothing at load time (hard-coded values, or getters). `window.R6` (`{<key>: <option key>}`, the baked round 6 picks, one option per step, defined once at the top of `round6.html`) and `window.SHAPES` exist by then.

- `q`: the question. `why`: why it is asked, with the Zero Hour default where one exists. `look`: what each option looks like, in words (it goes into the reference doc; the page labels it "What changes:").
- `rec`: the option key Claude recommends, one of the step's option keys (`A`, `B`; a draw-and-confirm step has only `A`, so `A`); anything else is an error banner and an audit line `CAND6 <key> rec invalid`. `recWhy`: one line why. A baked `R6[key]` wins over `rec` for the opening option and the picked pictures.
- An option may carry `names: [eight strings]` (the owner slot names of its `OWNERS` order, for a palette that reorders slots): the `view.palette` line-up and the audit lines label columns with them (today's names when absent), the export carries them as `palette.owner_names` when an `owners` option is baked; anything other than eight non-empty strings is an error banner and an audit line `CAND6 <key> <A|B> names invalid`.
- `uses: ['owners']` (optional): the step's stage (page, step shots, today and picked pictures) also applies the listed steps' options, for display only: the user's recorded pick in `picks.json` if it has one, else that step's `rec` (a baked `R6` pick first when there is no recorded one). The used step's data is never exported or audited as part of this step, but a `CARGO` option of a step with `uses` is also audited against the used option's `OWNERS` (owner vs cargo colour >= 20, with an `INFO` line for the smallest). A step key that is not another step is `CAND6 <key> uses invalid`.
- A draw-and-confirm step (only `A`) is answered `yes` or `change` with a note; it is not an option key.
- `numbers`: a string or an array of lines. `view`: options added to the base scene: `cam`, `focus`, `anchor`, `focusKey`, `dmg`, `mix`, `noCrates`, `owner`, `pair`, `palette: true` (an eight-owner line-up instead of the base scene: per owner one building, tank, light vehicle and infantry, plus a tank and infantry in a fog band across the bottom third, sand on the left half and the dark ground on the right, with the owner names and hex codes under each column; it uses the option's `OWNERS`, `paletteDark` and `DARK_GROUND`, today's colours for the `today` picture; zoom 1 by default, 0.5 works), and `place: [{id, cell: [x, y], owner?, heading?, turret?, shadow?}]` (a world cell, heading 0 to 7, `turret` a whole bucket 0 to 63 for the turret heading independent of the hull, only for a shape with a turret part, else an error and an audit line; `shadow: true` draws the unit shadow, default false; a building is centred on its cell), which draws a new `_wreck` or `_paused` shape where the option has one. A `place` entry without a `cell`, or with another kind of id, is an error banner and an audit line.
- `pics`: a view for the kept pictures, `{zoom: 0.5|1|2, today: view, picked: view}`, each view `view`-shaped plus an optional `show`: `pics: {zoom: 1, today: {focus: [14, 22]}, picked: {focus: [14, 22]}}` makes `<key>-today-z1` and `<key>-picked-z1` (underscores in the key become hyphens). Its `zoom` is also the default zoom of the key's `step<n>` shots.

`data` has four optional fields (`shapes`, `palette`, `paletteDark`, `overlay`), applied in the page, the pictures, the audit and the export:

- `shapes: {<id>: <shape>}` replaces whole shapes in the export's shape format. A replaced or new shape starts from the picked entry of its base id in `docs/images/map-art/shapes-picked.json`; `parts_by_detail` and `damage_variants` are what you give (none given: none), so copy them unless the option means to change them. An id is a shape of `shapes-current.json`, `pile_<big|small>_<100|60|25|5|0>`, `site_frame_<2|3>_<10|50|90>`, `salvage_crate`, or new: `<unit id>_wreck` (the base must be a unit) or `<id>_paused` (the base must be a building, or a construction site frame of the export's extras such as `site_frame_3_50`, giving `site_frame_3_50_paused`). New ids are drawn by the page's shape code, audited (paint order, footprint) and exported in `extras`.
- `palette: {<NAME>: value}`: `SIDE`, `SIDE_DARK`, `SHADOW`, `GROUND_SHADOW_RADIUS`, `SHADOW_RADIUS`, `SHADOW_FLAT`, `BUILDING_SHADOW_GROW`, `BUILDING_SHADOW_OFFSET`, `FOG`, `WRECK_TOP`, `WRECK_SIDE`, `WRECK_SIDE_DARK` (for `_wreck` shapes the page paints top faces, the lit side faces and the dark side faces with them in place of the machine and side band slots; the smoke slot 5 keeps its colour; each applies only when set; the export carries them as `palette.wreck`; the audit prints `INFO` contrast and colour-vision lines for `WRECK_TOP` against the grounds, fogged grounds and the owner tops, no bar decided), `DARK_GROUND` (the dark ground tone, today `#7B7566`; it drives the dark ground on the stage, in the audit and as `dark_ground` in the export palette), `OWNERS` (the eight owner colours, owner 1 to 8, in any order; the `owners` step's palette), `CARGO`, `PILE_PAD` (slot 5 of the pile and crate colours), `DOOR` (slot 7 of a building's colours), `BUILDING_SIDE_DARK` (slot 3 of a building's colours), and `CANVAS`, `EDGE_SIDE`, `EDGE_HEIGHT`, which only an overlay reads (`api.palette`). `paletteDark` takes the same names and applies only while the ground is dark; the export carries both as given.
- `overlay: (ctx, scene, api) => void` draws after the world on the base scene, for what is neither shape nor palette (the map edge, a no-power cue). `scene = {zoom, ground, items, origin, fogEdge}`; `api = {project(cellX, cellY) -> stage px, palette, ground, dark, zoom, lighten, darken, mix, fillPoly, slotsFor}`. The export does not carry it; describe it in `look`.

One step owns each shape id and palette name: two steps setting the same one is an error banner and an audit line `CAND6 <key> owns clash`. A key that is not a step is `CAND6 <key> key unknown`.

`#audit` runs the default-set checks (always) and every option of every step: paint order and footprint for the shapes it replaces, owner share for unit shapes; building side and band contrast (3) for what it changes; and for an option that sets `OWNERS`, `DARK_GROUND` or `CARGO` the palette as a whole (owner order may change), on the sand set and the dark-ground set in force: every owner pair colour vision (`minDistance`: normal, deuteranopia, protanopia) >= 20, every owner against the HUD slab >= 3 and against the cargo colour in force and the HUD amber `#F7C871` >= 20, side band and dark side band against the ground in force >= 3, and per ground (sand, dark ground, fogged sand and fogged dark ground at 0.65) the minimum over owners of the owner-to-ground distance not below today's minimum on that ground (bounds in `DE_FROM`, floored to 0.01; there is no owner-top contrast bar against a ground, the bar is not decided, user 2026-11-17). Each palette's per-ground minimum prints as an `INFO` line. A `CARGO` option adds cargo contrast (3) and cargo against the HUD amber (20). Lines are prefixed `CAND6 <key> <A|B> `. `sh report.sh 'audit&name=audit-<key>&step=<key>' audit-<key>` limits the step part to your step (no clash check, nothing of other steps; a missing file is `FAIL`); the last line is `PASS <checks> known <k>` or `FAIL <n>`. Once picks are baked in `R6`, the default audit reads the baked cargo, owner and ground colours, including `paletteDark`, `DARK_GROUND` and a `paletteDark` `FOG`; a baked `OWNERS` option is judged as a whole by the same palette rule as an option (per-ground minima as `INFO`, the baked `names`), so the per-owner distance KNOWN entries apply only while `R6.owners` is empty, and the cargo KNOWN entries (sand 2.44, pink 10.9) only while `R6.cargo` is empty.
