# Faction guide round pictures

Source for the War Room round `faction-guide` (P21, 2026-11-27). `guide.html` draws the Faction guide over the dimmed match world for the Esc entry (as `../esc-round/esc.html`) and over `../lobby-look-round/lobby-look.html` for the lobby entry, with hash or query parameters: `view` (`usa`, `china`, `frc`: the guide from the Esc menu, where you play USA, on that tab; `guide`: on `faction`; `menu`: the Esc menu with Faction guide; `lobby`: the lobby with its Guide button; `lobby-guide`: the guide over the lobby, opened on your pick; `lobby-random`: you picked Random, so it opens on USA and nothing is marked yours), `layout` (step 1: `list`, `table`, `cards`), `counters` (step 2: `class`, `named`, `marks`), `f44` (step 3: `print`, `muted`, `fix`; `fix` draws a note, since its numbers do not exist yet), `faction` (`usa`, `china`, `frc`: the tab for `view=guide` and your pick in the lobby states, default China there), `size` (`1280x720`, `1280x800`, `1920x1080`; the UI scales from 1280 x 720 as the game does); the defaults are the round's recommendations, and `measure=1` prints the measurement rows on the page (panel size, fit with a 16 px margin, the tab row's top, pinned at 24 px, text cut off, smallest text, and in the lobby whether Guide fits its column and the faction dropdowns line up).

Sizes and colours come from `game/ui/game_theme.tres` (HudSmallPanel, HudSmallButton, HudSmallToggle, HudLabel, HudTitle, HudHint), the guide panel opaque with an owner-colour edge (`palette.gd` OWNER_BASE blue); unit pictures from `../hud-v5/source/glyphs3.js` as `unit_glyphs.gd` maps them. Every number is copied from `game/scripts/simulation/definitions/` (build time = ticks / 30, rounded up; health = max_hp / 1000; units without a `vision` key show a dash), and the page computes every counter with test CL1's equal-credit time-to-kill math (`game/tests/test_hud.gd`), keeping only Beats (ratio at most 0.5) and Weak against (at least 2); the class lines are `HudModel.COUNTER_LINES` verbatim. The Rotary Gun Tank's 1.6 s spin-up is test R1's interval pin (`game/tests/test_ramp.gd`: 12, 12, 6, 6, 6, 6, then 4 ticks). `node docs/images/faction-guide-round/check_counters.js` checks that for all 144 pairs a unit lists another under Beats exactly when that one lists it under Weak against (exit 1 otherwise). The lobby states add the Guide button to the lobby-look page from outside and widen its action column to 64 px. It needs the repository layout (`game/fonts/`); serve it through War Room.

The `.webp` files are lossless 2x exports (2560 x 1440) from `sh warroom/tools/shoot.sh`: `layout-*` (step 1 options on USA), `counters-*` (step 2 options on China), `f44-muted` (step 3 option B), `entry-menu`, `entry-lobby`, `lobby-guide` and `lobby-random`.
