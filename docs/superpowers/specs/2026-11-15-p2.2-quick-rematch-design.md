# Quick rematch, slice 1: design (P2.2)

Status: draft for approval (2026-11-15). Decisions marked (user, 2026-11-15) were answered in the P2.2 design questionnaires; the agent's remaining calls are listed at the end. Scope and riders live in the [P2.2 row](../../packages.md#day-lane); the research is Grok's hidden-tab note (private, `hidden-tab-pings.md`).

## Why

Two friends should be able to play several matches from one link. Today the host cannot switch apps to send the link (a waiting lobby is cut after 10 s of silence), a match ends with no way back to the same opponent (the client closes the socket, the relay keeps `started` forever), and nothing caps how long a stranger's socket or an unjoined lobby may sit on the relay.

## Goals

- A lobby waiting for player 2 survives minutes of silence and ends after about 15 min with "Your friend did not join in time".
- A fresh socket that never sends `hello` is cut after 5 s.
- A host whose tab is hidden when the friend joins is not dropped at match start (F11): the match waits for them.
- After a match both players can press **Play again**: same room, same opponent, same map and factions, new seed, no new link.
- Result panel with outcome, Play again, Change setup; Enter is Play again. Online and offline.
- Launcher: Enter submits the join code; elapsed time and Cancel (button and Esc) while connecting, leaving no half-open relay session.

## Non-goals

- Slice 2: match length, units built/lost, buildings lost, and the other launcher riders (default focus, focus outline, 32 px targets, 12 px Tag, Quit, invite-field label).
- Protocol (WebSocket) pings: still a gated rider after this slice; this slice uses deadlines only. The hidden-tab check stays a manual, attended step at the playtest.
- Changing map or faction between matches (Change setup closes the room; a new link is needed). Word codes, reconnecting into a running match, the M9 lobby.

## Anti-goals

- The 10 s silence rule stays for running matches: a seat that has sent its first tick and goes quiet is still announced as `left` after 10 s.
- No change to simulation, replay or trace: every rematch is a fresh `start`, fresh `Lockstep` and fresh `Simulation`.
- No lobby or parked room can be held open forever by pings.

## Approaches

1. **Reset the room in place (chosen).** The room keeps its sockets, code and settings; two new client messages park it and restart it. Smallest change to how clients connect, fits "no new link", and the relay already owns seat state in one goroutine.
2. **Client-driven restart.** Clients restart locally and the relay is told nothing. Rejected: the relay's per-seat `lastTick` order check (`room.forward`) rejects a new match's tick 4 as invalid, so the relay must learn about the restart anyway.
3. **New room, code handed over the old socket.** Host creates a room and the relay forwards the code. Rejected: needs a relay-to-client "invite" message and a join round trip for what is a state reset; also leaves a window where the friend can be lost between rooms.

## Design

### Relay (`relay/`)

Deadlines, env-tunable like `WARGRID_SILENCE_MS`: `WARGRID_HELLO_MS` 5000, and one `WARGRID_LOBBY_MS` 900000 for all three long waits (lobby expiry, start window, park limit), so conformance shortens them with one variable. "Every seat" below means all `cfg.seats` seats, present at the same time; a room that lost a seat cannot rematch.

| Phase | Read deadline | Renewed by |
|---|---|---|
| Socket open, no hello | hello deadline, 5 s | nothing (a frame before a valid hello does not extend it) |
| Lobby (`!started`, fewer than `cfg.seats` seated) | room expiry, 15 min after room creation | nothing: JSON pings and protocol pongs do not extend it |
| Started, before this seat's first tick | start window, 15 min (user, 2026-11-15) | the seat's first tick switches it to the next row |
| Started, seat has sent a tick | silence, 10 s | every frame, as today |
| Seat sent `over`, others not yet | silence, 10 s (the others report within moments of the same tick; a silent one is dropped as today) | every frame |
| Parked (all seats sent `over`) | park limit, 15 min after parking (user, 2026-11-15) | nothing |

`OnMessage` today refreshes the deadline before it looks at the frame; it becomes state-aware so a frame before hello renews nothing and an `over`-parked room is not renewed by pings.

- **Lobby expiry** is a timer in `room.run` while `!started`. On fire the room sends `{"type":"error","reason":"lobby expired"}` and closes the seat (the client turns that reason into the did-not-join text instead of "Connection to relay lost"). The same path with reason `room expired` covers a parked room.
- **Start window:** when the room sends `start` (first match and every rematch) it sets each seat's deadline to the start window; the seat's first accepted tick drops it to the 10 s rule.
- **`over`** (client to relay, `{"type":"over"}`, (user, 2026-11-15)): valid only in a started room, only once per match per seat, and only after that seat sent at least one accepted tick in this match (so a room cannot be cycled through `over` and `again` without playing). Once every seat has sent it, the room is *parked*. A seat that sent `over` and then sends a `tick` is rejected as `invalid message`.
- **`again`** (client to relay `{"type":"again"}`, relay to client `{"type":"again","seat":N}`, (user, 2026-11-15)): valid only after the seat's own `over`. The relay echoes it to the other seats, so a friend's click is visible on the panel. When every seat has sent `again`, the relay resets `lastTick`, `over` and `again` flags, picks a new seed and sends `start` (same seats, same delay, the room's stored settings) to everyone. `started` stays true and the room keeps its code.
- **Leave** during parked or rematch-pending state is the existing `leaveMsg` path: `left` goes to the remaining seats, who can no longer complete `again`. An empty room is removed as today (`MATCH END` per room, byte and frame counters accumulate across rematches; a `rematch` line is logged on each restart).
- **Drain:** a parked room closes when the relay drains, like an unstarted lobby; `again` is refused while draining.
- Bad ordering (`again` before `over`, `over` in a lobby, `hello` after seat) is `invalid message`, which closes the socket as today.

### Wire contract (user approval, 2026-11-15)

Three message uses, each with a vector in `protocol/vectors` and conformance cases: client `over`, client `again`, relay `again` with `seat`. `error.reason` gains the strings `lobby expired` and `room expired`; the `error` shape is unchanged. `welcome` is unchanged (the countdown is a client constant). The existing cases `unknown_type`, `room_code_freed_after_empty`, `started_room_stays_full_after_leave` and the exact-`welcome` cases stay as they are. `silence_announces_left` (`conform.go:360`) moves: seat 1 never ticks, which now falls under the 15 min start window, so the case makes seat 1 send one tick first. The plan lists new cases for hello timeout, lobby expiry, pings not extending expiry, the start window, the 10 s rule after the first tick, `over`/`again` ordering and the rematch round trip.

### Client (`game/scripts/`)

- **`relay_transport.gd`**: `silent()` takes its limit from a `patient` setting: 5 s normally, relaxed (matching the relay's lobby deadline) while waiting in the lobby, before the first tick of a match, and while the result panel is up. A hidden tab that stops `_process` no longer trips the rule on return.
- **`launcher.gd` Waiting view:** countdown from a client constant (`LOBBY_EXPIRY_SEC`, 900) mirroring the relay default (user, 2026-11-15); reason `lobby expired` maps to "Your friend did not join in time"; "Connection to relay lost" stays for real losses.
- **Connecting:** the status shows elapsed seconds and a Cancel button (Esc also cancels) that closes the transport and returns to the page with picks kept; Enter in the join-code field joins.
- **`online_driver.gd`:** on a sim-decided end (won, draw) it sends `over` and keeps the transport open instead of closing it; abnormal ends (desync, left, relay lost, protocol) still close and show today's notice with no rematch. `again` is sent by the panel; a relay `start` while parked builds a new `Lockstep` and `Simulation`. This is new wiring around `match_screen._restart`, which today is gated by `driver.can_restart()` (false online) and does not call `driver.start()`; the launcher currently owns `Lockstep` construction, so that moves into the driver. `Lockstep.receive` and `Wire.decode` end a match on unknown types, so `again` and the second `start` are decoded and handled by the driver before they reach the lockstep, and `Wire` learns `over` and `again`.
- **Deploy order:** a new client against today's relay gets `invalid message` on `over`, so rematch only works once the relay change is deployed. The playtest therefore needs the relay test deploy (needs your explicit approval at that gate) before the web release; local runs use the local relay.
- **Result panel (online and offline):** a centred card over the dimmed world (user, 2026-11-15) replaces the Result label and Restart button: outcome (Victory, Defeat, Draw), a status line ("Waiting for your friend…", "Your friend wants to play again", "Your friend left"), **Play again** (primary, Enter) and **Change setup** (leaves, back to the launcher with picks kept). Offline Play again is today's restart. Play again is disabled once the friend has left or the room closed.
- **`headless_client.gd`** gains a rematch mode so `run_netplay.sh` can play two matches on one room.

### Not touched

`Simulation`, command formats, replays, the trace pin, `protocol` version (stays 2: additive messages, old clients never see them because only a new client sends `over`; a mixed-build room is already refused by `build mismatch`).

## Confirmed calls (user, 2026-11-15, second questionnaire)

- `over` is accepted only after that seat sent one accepted tick this match.
- A friend who leaves ends the room's rematch: Play again is disabled with "Your friend left"; a started room never reopens a seat.
- `MATCH END` counters add up across rematches; a `rematch` line is logged on each restart.
- Start positions re-roll from the new seed (existing `MatchSettings.starts`).
- Desync, left, relay lost and protocol endings get no rematch.
- Parked rooms close on relay drain, and `again` is refused while draining.
- A seat that sent `over` keeps the 10 s rule until every seat has.
- Error reasons `lobby expired` and `room expired` inside the existing `error` message; one `WARGRID_LOBBY_MS` for lobby expiry, start window and park limit; `WARGRID_HELLO_MS` for the hello deadline.

## Agent calls not asked

- The relay trusts a client's `over`: it cannot know a match really ended. A cheating client can only park its own seat; the room parks only when every seat has said so, so one liar cannot lift the 10 s rule for a running opponent.
- Status-line wording on the panel and the Waiting view.
- The panel dims the world and Enter is Play again only while it is enabled; Esc keeps opening the pause menu.
- The second button reads **Back to menu** instead of "Change setup" (user, 2026-11-15, after the playtest: "Change setup" was confusing for a second); same action, node and signal names unchanged.
