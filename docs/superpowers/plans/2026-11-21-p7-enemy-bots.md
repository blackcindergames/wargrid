## Why

The goal is a 2v2 with a friend against bots (user, 2026-11-20). Today's bot sends its whole army once at 180 s; the first two waves cancel out; then 6-unit waves lose 84-97 % and reach a building 13-20 % of the time (`BL`). Payoff: two friends hold a combined push together, then counter-attack together. Part 1 is the simulation half of `PK:28`; part 2 (2v2 online) waits for part 1 and night 8. **Out:** allied bot (`PK:77`), P8, standoff, tech escalation, bot levels (`BP` S4 on).

**Decided before 2026-11-21 (do not reopen):** director-round bot rules, one wave clock and one target per team (user, 2026-11-20, `DA:1443`); fairness: same rules, no `next_random()` in a decision (`BP`); bot units on human unit rules (`DA:1124`); new tests use only the simulation interface, `test_skirmish_waves.gd` is allow-listed.

### Design

**Evidence:** the bot is money-limited, not idle (affordable-unit idle 4 %, credits ≥ 1,000 in under 1 % of samples, `BL`); +1 collector won 19 of 28 decided matches, and 7 of 28 ending matches stopped ending (private `better-bots/README.md:24,83`).

**Rules** (constants in `SK`; BP S4 later moves them to the skill profile). 30 ticks per second. A *live bot* is a seat whose bot has decided at least once (`decide` creates its `BOT_META` entry before any gate; today only `_attack` does, `SK:177,181-184`) and that still owns a building with `counts_for_victory` in `bot_state().buildings` (the `_survives` rule, `simulation.gd:178-181`). A unit is *ready* as in `_gather` today: idle guard (`_idle`, `SK:238`), in no wave, not sent by `_defend`, within `RALLY_RADIUS` of its own start.
- **BR1 Economy:** collectors per supply centre 2/3/5 → 3/4/6 (`definitions/factions.gd:12,20,28`).
- **BR2 Timetable:** wave 0 due at `FIRST_WAVE_TICK` 5,400 (4,500 until kill check B); wave n+1 due `WAVE_GAPS[n]` after wave n leaves: 2,700 after waves 0 and 1, 1,800 from wave 2 on. Replaces `ATTACK_TICK`, `WAVE_MIN`, `WAVE_TIMEOUT` (`SK:12,14,15,220`); `DEFENCE_RADIUS` stays. A caller's `attack_tick` overrides `FIRST_WAVE_TICK` for that bot's team, and `_attack`/`_gather` stay gated until the team's first due tick, so ScriptedHuman's staging (`scripted_human.gd:34-43`) never fights rally moves (`R1`). Nothing ready at the wait cap: the wave leaves at the first decision with a ready unit (as today's `since = -1`, `SK:216-219`).
- **BR3 Capped growing waves, in cost:** a unit's cost is `cost / quantity` (Militia 150, `china.gd:11`). `UNIT_COST`: integer mean of that over the faction's `units_for` kinds (from definitions); a team's: integer mean over its live bots. Target = (`WAVE_SIZES[n]` × live bots + `DEF_UNITS` × BR6's counted defences) × team `UNIT_COST`; `WAVE_SIZES` 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, then 32. A wave leaves when the summed cost of all the team's ready units reaches the target, or at `WAVE_WAIT` 1,800 after due with what is ready. The take: ready units, highest unit cost first, ties lowest id, until the summed cost first reaches the target (that unit included), never skipping one. (Since 2026-11-22: vehicles and infantry in turn, see "After the owner's BR8 match".) Wave 0 also takes at most `WAVE0_UNITS` 16 per team and gets no BR6 raise (BR6 only retargets it). The rest guards home (`_defend`, `SK:241`) and joins the next wave; late arrivals wait. Uncapped, today's infantry-heavy mix (first-wave mean unit cost about 310-361, `BL` raw rows) would put 20-23 units in a two-bot wave 0.
- **BR4 One clock per team:** `BOT_TEAM_META` on `_real(sim)` (as `SK:182`, so `CommandRecorder` tests see it), keyed by team: `{wave, due, released_by, launched_at, objective, attacked_at: {player: tick}, left_cost, left_units, joiners}`. The first team bot deciding at or after `due` sums the team's ready cost from `bot_state()` (reuse `_gather`'s rally test; new per-unit maths in `[x, y]` floats, `determinism-rules.md:12`). Once met (or at the wait cap) it launches, taking its own ready units (BR3 order) up to `target / live bots` (wave 0: and `16 / live bots` units), and stores what is left. Each live teammate joins at its next decision (within 29 ticks, `SK:81`), in decision order, taking up to `left / joiners still to come` (the last one up to `left`). Integer division; the remainder goes to the last joiner. A lone bot is a team of one. `released_by`: *timer* = met at the first team decision at or after `due`; *size* = met later, before `due + WAVE_WAIT`; *wait cap* = left short at `due + WAVE_WAIT`.
- **BR5 One objective per team:** target the live enemy player with the lowest `attacked_at` (never attacked = -1; ties lowest id), written when a wave launches at that player's building; then today's score (`_pick`, `SK:262`) among that player's buildings from the team's mean rally. Objective dies: the first bot to notice re-picks from its wave's centre and stores it. The engaged re-pick (`SK:194-198,276`) never writes the team objective; when its unit dies the wave returns to the team objective.
- **BR6 Defences in the way, in cost (waves 1 on):** a completed enemy armed building (priority 29, `SK:287`) within `DEF_REACH` of the objective, or of a cell every 8 cells on the line team mean rally → objective, counts once and raises the target by `DEF_UNITS` × team `UNIT_COST`. Unmet by `WAVE_WAIT` (wave 0 included): retarget to the best enemy building with no counted defence, the objective's player first, then by BR5's player order; none, go anyway. No retreat, no standoff (`ZH§4`).
- **UR1 Human unit rules:** pass `"human"` at `SK:53` (`second_controller` default), `match/offline_driver.gd:32`, `debug/team_match.gd:17`, `lab/readme_capture.gd:50`. Difference: a human idle guard scans weapon reach, the bot max(vision × 1.333, reach) (`combat.gd:64,77-78,170-171,178-179`). Records gain `controller` (writer `match/match_screen.gd:194` `_header`; key pin `test_playtest.gd:347`); `tools/playtest_session.py:118-128` emits it in `--replay-args`, `tools/replay_session.sh:27-29` reads it and passes `--controller`; the oracle `net/headless_client.gd:149` uses it, missing → `"ai"`. `benchmarks/run_recorded_match.gd:33` keeps `"ai"` (`BP` S3).

**Probe** (scratch, durable copy in `~/projects/wargrid-private/docs/better-bots/probe/p7/`, `ctrl=human`): Dry Wells and Ashfall sets as `BL`, plus Broken Crown 2v2 teams `[1,1,2,2]`, both slot orders, 5 seeds, and 1v2 against an idle seat. Rows: minute-6 fighting units; wave-0 units per team; per wave the launch-to-launch gap and `released_by`; reach; idle-seat ticks to first building lost and to defeat; no-winner count; night 12's two columns (`PK:61`) against the stock rows of T1-1. **7-13 floor:** per map, the idle seat's defeat tick under the new rules, 1v1 and 1v2, is at least the stock 1v1 minimum on that map: Dry Wells 7,414, Ashfall 2,6 10,698, Ashfall 3,7 10,314 (`BL`), Broken Crown from T1-1. **150 s rule:** if `released_by` is *timer* for more than half of waves 1 on across the matrix, rerun with `WAVE_GAPS` 4,500 throughout from a scratch worktree (constants are `const`; never committed) and report both rows.
